# Source and Claim Policy

Use this reference when deciding whether a source is strong enough for an NZ Study marketing claim.

| Claim type | Preferred evidence |
|---|---|
| Facility exists, campus count, programme, fee, date, service | Official institution page or official document |
| Accreditation, visa, regulatory status | Government, regulator, accreditor |
| Ranking or award | Original ranking body or award issuer |
| "Known for", reputation, comparison, "leading", "best" | Independent reputable source that explicitly supports the comparison |
| Student experience | Official student survey or clearly attributed first-person/student source |
| Local transport, city facts | Government, transport operator, official statistics, reputable local authority |

Search snippets, AI summaries, reposts, and scraped copies are discovery aids. Open the underlying source before treating the claim as verified.

A source can verify that an institution **says** something about itself. It does not automatically verify that the statement is an independent fact about its reputation.
